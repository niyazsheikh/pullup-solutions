Welcome to PULL UP Business Solutions ("PULL UP", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, enquiry forms, consultation requests, and other communication channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our website or services.

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

Business Information

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

PULL UP does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal proceedings, or lawful government requests.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, we expect appropriate safeguards to be maintained.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website traffic, and enhance user experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

If analytics or advertising tools are enabled, their use will be subject to applicable privacy requirements and relevant third-party policies.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information shared with us against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided below.

PULL UP is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. We encourage visitors to review their policies before sharing personal information.

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals capable of entering into professional engagements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in connection with our business services.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances where applicable law requires additional safeguards, we will take appropriate action.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, website functionality, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page along with the revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
